Play-money poker lobby
======================

qbit-qt has a new **Window → Poker Lobby** entry: no-limit Texas Hold'em
against computer players at heads-up, 6-max and full-ring tables. Chips are
play money with no value. The game never touches the wallet, sends nothing over
the network, and does nothing until the lobby is opened.

The lobby and table follow the two poker windows Satoshi Nakamoto designed for
the first Bitcoin client, which were never connected to a game and were removed
in 2009. See `doc/user/poker.md`.
